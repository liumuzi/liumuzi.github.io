# 创始团队介绍 Pitch Deck - Muzi Liu

## 🇨🇳 中文版 (Chinese Version)

**刘木子 Muzi Liu  - 游戏 CEO、主程及独立制作人**

毕业于**香港中文大学人工智能专业**，拥有逾** 4 年**跨越** AI 算法与全栈游戏开发**的复合经验。她致力于将前沿的大语言模型（LLM）与强化学习技术同创新玩法深度融合，为玩家打造充满动态变数与即兴叙事的游戏体验。

在技术底座方面，她曾担任顶尖大厂**米哈游**（《原神》）的核心图形算法工程师，主导开发了全局 **AI 动画生成管线**，实现了 90% 以上面部资产的全自动产出。这段工业级的研发实战，赋予了她坚实的底层技术架构能力，确保能够将高难度 AI 机制完美、稳定地落地。

作为具备技术与设计双重视角的跨界开发者，她在多个独立游戏项目中担纲制作人，精通从 0 到 1 的全链路开发。她主导的作品在**全球最大 Game Jam**（GMTK 2025）中斩获**叙事榜 Top 1%**（#141）。此外，她还带队跑通了多线敏捷开发：一方面，她从零建制并带领团队研发的《虫虫家装》荣获**光子游戏大赛2026概念赛道银奖**；另一方面，亲自操刀的《我的恋综超失控》屡获行业认可，揽获了**腾讯游戏创作大赛“AI 玩法创作奖”**与**抖音青芽游戏创作大赛“AI 游戏奖”**，并入选了 **CUSGA2026（“怪好玩奖”入围）**及 **AICON2026 亚洲地区展览**。这些实战成绩证明了她在团队管理、项目统筹与商业化落地上的强劲实力。

## 🇺🇸 英文版 (English Version)

**Muzi Liu - CEO, Lead Programmer and Indie Producer**

Holding a B.S. in **Artificial Intelligence from CUHK**, Muzi brings over **4 years** of hybrid expertise spanning **AI algorithms and full-stack game development**. She specializes in integrating cutting-edge LLMs and reinforcement learning with innovative gameplay to craft highly dynamic and unpredictable narrative experiences.

With a robust industrial technical foundation, she formerly served as a **core Algorithm Engineer at top-tier studio miHoYo** (*Genshin Impact*), where she spearheaded an **AI animation pipeline** that achieved over 90% automation in facial asset production. This rigorous background guarantees the seamless technical execution and stability of her current project's complex "improvised AI interactions."

Bridging technology and game design, Muzi acts as a Producer on several independent titles, mastering the end-to-end development cycle. She led a team to create a narrative puzzle game that ranked in the **Top 1% globally for Narrative at GMTK Game Jam 2025** (out of nearly 10,000 entries). Furthermore, she has proven agile management capabilities across distinct projects: she built and led the team behind *Bug Décor*, winner of the **Silver Award in the Concept Track at the Photon Game Competition 2026**; while her current flagship project, *My Dating Show is Out of Control*, has garnered multiple industry accolades, including the **AI Gameplay Innovation Award at the Tencent Game Innovation Contest**, the **AI Game Award at Douyin's Budding Game Innovation Contest**, a nomination for the Weirdly Fun Award at **CUSGA 2026**, and an official selection for the **AICON 2026 Asia Regional Exhibition**. These achievements underscore her exceptional project execution and commercialization leadership.
